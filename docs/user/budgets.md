# Budgets

Under the **Budget** tab.

## A figure per category per month

Set what you intend to spend on a category in a month; MyFinance shows what actually
happened against it, as a meter with the numbers written beside it. The whole year is
available as a single view as well.

The numbers are always written out. A bar you have to judge by eye is not a figure.

## Rollover

A category can carry what it did not spend into next month.

**An overspend is never carried forward.** Next month starts from the figure you set for it.
Carrying a deficit compounds a bad month into a worse one and makes the budget a record of
failure rather than a plan.

## Copy across the year

A budget is mostly the same number twelve times, and typing it out twelve times is how
people give up on budgeting. Copying fills the year from one month.

**Months that already have a figure are left alone**, so this is safe to use again after you
have adjusted a few of them.
